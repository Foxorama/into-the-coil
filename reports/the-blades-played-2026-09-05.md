## What was built, that this is a verdict on

A blade was thrown at 1.4 units' radius (a 3.2-unit star against a 7.6-unit ship), opened its
spiral at 0.28 units a step, and was spent by its own clock — 60 to 120 steps by rung — wherever
that left it: 17 to 34 units out, a third of the way to the edge of the lane. The picture was a
ring of small stars close about the ship, ending in mid-air.
