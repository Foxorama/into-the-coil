# The coil drawn — 2026-09-05

After the fifth play-test, the player traced the path they wanted the blades to take over a
screenshot of the bench, and sent it with the words below. Every item is answered in
[0242](../docs/decisions/0242-a-blade-coils-ahead-of-the-ship.md); this file is the record of what
was said and drawn.

## What was said

> *"ignore the bullets and just look at the traced path, what do you think of that for the
> shuriken paths? starting from the wing tips and circling forwards. the spiral whirlpool we have
> is cool, but it feels really weird and has a lot of hard to control gaps still"*

Then, asked whether the two blades of a pair should cross in front of the nose and whether a
blade's edge should stay at twelve landings:

> *"cross the blades in front of the nose, how fast will max tier blades kill a boss? It's probably
> ok to leave as is because you'll need to be on target to hit a boss without getting free hits like
> you do now with the whirlpool"*

## What was drawn

A chain of overlapping loops running from the ship's wingtips to the leading edge of the screen,
two tracks braided across the ship's line: loops about fourteen units tall, each advancing about
twenty-two units on the last, so each overlaps the last by a third. The path of a point circling a
centre that moves up the lane — a trochoid — drawn twice, a half-turn out of phase.

## What was built, that this is a verdict on

A blade circled a ring centred thirty units ahead of the ship and stretched along the lane
([0240](../docs/decisions/0240-the-blades-reach-the-boss.md)), leaving the ship's nose and spiralling
out to the edge of the screen. The pitch between the spiral's arms was the gap; it depended on where
the ship sat in the lane and where in its turn a blade met an edge.
