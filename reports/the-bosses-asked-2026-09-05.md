# The bosses asked for — 2026-09-05

The seventh play-test, given as a brief for the night: the current bosses become mid-bosses, and
every place gets a real one. The first answer is
[0247](../docs/decisions/0247-a-level-has-a-mid-boss-and-a-real-one.md); the rest are the
decisions it lists as owed, one per boss. This file is the record of what was said, in full,
because every one of those decisions will be written against it.

> we can get them, but I expect we'll need to refine and improve them."*

> *"the end boss of Ember Nebula is going to be some hell-spawned demon space eagle thing that

> *"Saurian Belt is going to be a flying pteradactyl with lazers mounted on it's wings and it opens
> it's mouth to fire a huge laser blast. the current end boss needs to be moved to the mid-boss of
> the labyrinth and the labyrinth current end boss will be a mid-tier flying dino in the saurian
> belt."*

## What was played, that this is a verdict on

Seven bosses, one per level, at the end of each: the sentinel, the harrow, the lattice, the shoal
mother, the redoubt, the chorus and the axis — each one idea, on a vocabulary of three flights and
five fans, two of them with a curtain and an eye
([0111](../docs/decisions/0111-a-boss-has-one-idea.md),
[0150](../docs/decisions/0150-the-uncoil-and-the-eye.md),
[0151](../docs/decisions/0151-the-gap-you-have-to-reach.md)).

## What the items are

- **The structure**: two fights a level — a mid-boss, then a real one.
- **The roster**: the seven become the mid-bosses at half health, two of them moved between places;
  seven real bosses, one per place, each named and described.
- **The vocabulary the real bosses need and the game does not have**: acid, void, flame, frost and
  laser shots; a rain from the top of the screen with warning lines; a whip of fire; a beam from a
  wing and a huge one from a mouth; summoned adds through a fight; a wall that spins; a cold that
  slows and freezes the player; a boss that grows heads; tendrils that pulse lightning; adds that
  rain down; a final phase that opens and spews.
