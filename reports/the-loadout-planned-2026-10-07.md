## What was true

Photographed every ship in every gun at none, one and two tubes, at seven times the shipped size:

- **The fighter's own gun is a pair of cigar pods past its wingtips**, wider than the wings; a borrowed gun is
  painted last, on the canopy and the nose, over the nose art; its one tube sits on the chin over the art.
- **The cars' borrowed guns stand on the bonnet**; the Thunderbolt's on the fork crown, against the rider's
  hands. Every tube is the same drawing whatever it fires.
- **The hangar pad never draws tubes**: `paintBlue` bakes stage 0.

## The queue

1. **The loadout has tabs.** Hangin' Out's bands behind sub-tabs, one group in view; the Tubes band moves from
   Paint & Parts to *Loadout*, and Paint & Parts' headings come back on phones.
2. **The key opens.** Each pickup face on How to play is a control a tap, a click, a pad and the keys reach;
   it opens a sheet with what it is and what it gives.
3. **The weapons sit right.** Every ship's guns and tubes placed where they read and hide nothing — the
   fighter's guns on the nose ahead of its art, its own pulse among them; its tubes under the wings — and every
   tube in its kind's ink, so a mixed rack reads as one of each.
4. **The pad wears its tubes.** The hangar's ship baked with the rack fitted, in their inks.
