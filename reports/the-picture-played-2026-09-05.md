# The picture played — 2026-09-05

The second play-test of the new weapons, on the branch previews of
[0235](../docs/decisions/0235-a-seeker-hunts-the-nearest-body.md) and
[0237](../docs/decisions/0237-the-blades-answer-the-first-play-test.md), the same day they were
built. Every item is answered in
[0238](../docs/decisions/0238-the-picture-answers-the-second-play-test.md); this file is the record
of what was said.

## The items

**The missiles.**

> *"need more visual distinction between actual missile types"*

**The lightning.**

> *"lightning needs more glow around the edges, not specific details but more like the lightning
> flash etc"*

**The blades.**

> *"shurikens need to be bigger and steel coloured, also with a bit of a glow to them"*

## What was built, that this is a verdict on

A seeker was a 3.4-unit dart in the pulse's orange, told from the 3.4-unit straight missile by
swept fins and an eye. A bolt was three strokes: a dark halo six cores wide, a glow three cores
wide at a third of the alpha, and the core. A blade was a 7-unit star in the pulse's orange with a
dark centre and no light on it.
