# The guns played again — 2026-09-05

The third play-test of the new weapons, on the branch preview of
[0238](../docs/decisions/0238-the-picture-answers-the-second-play-test.md), the same day it was
built. Every item is answered in
[0239](../docs/decisions/0239-the-guns-answer-the-third-play-test.md); this file is the record of
what was said.

## The items

**The spiral.**

> *"shurikens need a slightly tighter spiral, there's too much gap at the moment."*

**The arc's reach, and its joins.**

> *"lightning is a little bit long at max power, it's a bit OP - it also needs some bright white
> dots at the centre points of the joins to really lift it, but otherwise looks good"*

**The missile pickups.**

> *"and the missile pickups need to be different colours and have a much different appearance,
> they look incredibly similar at the moment"*

**The weapon pickups.**

> *"weapon pickups need different colouration for each weapon as well, visually distinct atm but
> the same colour makes it hard"*

## What was built, that this is a verdict on

A blade gained 40 units outward per turn at the first rung and 15 at the cap. The arc reached 114
units at the cap, two thirds of the narrowest view, with a dot on every third vertex at under
twice the core's width and a four-pointed spark where a link landed. Every face of every pickup
was in the pickup ink; the seeker's face was the missile's chevron with a hole through it.
