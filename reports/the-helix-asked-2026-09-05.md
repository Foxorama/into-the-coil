# The helix asked for — 2026-09-05

The sixth play-test of the shuriken, on the branch preview of
[0242](../docs/decisions/0242-a-blade-coils-ahead-of-the-ship.md) and
[0243](../docs/decisions/0243-a-death-throws-back-one-piece-per-kind.md). Every item is answered
in [0244](../docs/decisions/0244-a-blade-rides-a-helix.md); this file is the record of what was
said.

## What was said

> *"ok this one is on me, the shurikens are feeling better but I didn't describe what I wanted
> properly. I want the two wingtips firing to form a helix pattern with the shurikens, and the
> shurikens need to be slightly faster than they are now."*

Then, while it was being built:

> *"also the shuriken graphics need to be a bit smaller, they take up a lot of visual screenspace
> and make it hard to see enemies and enemy fire"*

## What was played, that this is a verdict on

A pair of blades from the wingtips, each circling a point that moved up the lane at 0.8 units a
step, so its track was a chain of loops — 0242's reading of the path the player had drawn over the
bench after the fifth play-test ([`the-coil-drawn`](the-coil-drawn-2026-09-05.md)). The star was
twelve units of a hundred-unit lane, 0238's size.

## What the items are

- **The shape**: two strands crossing each other up the lane — a helix seen from above — not loops.
  The drawing had been read as a trochoid; the word says a sine.
- **The pace**: *slightly* faster. The blade's forward speed was 0.8 a step.
- **The size**: smaller, for the sake of everything else on the screen — the enemies and their fire.
