# The blades played again — 2026-09-05

The fourth play-test of the new weapons, on the branch preview of
[0239](../docs/decisions/0239-the-guns-answer-the-third-play-test.md), the same day it was built.
Every item is answered in [0240](../docs/decisions/0240-the-blades-reach-the-boss.md); this file is
the record of what was said.

## The items

**The two orange faces.**

> *"autofire gun colour symbol needs to be more orangey and the regular fire missiles icon needs to
> be more orangey to match the projectiles."*

**The blades' reach.**

> *"shurikens need to stretch out a bit further as well, same original problem as the lightning, it
> doesn't reach far enough and as a result you have to get too close to bosses"*

## What was built, that this is a verdict on

The pulse's and the straight missile's pickup faces were in the pickup ink — the first face of
each pickup kept it so a fresh pickup read as one. A blade's ring was centred on the ship and
round; the ship flies about forty units from the edge of the screen behind it, so the ring left by
that edge at forty-odd units out, before it was fifty ahead, and a boss sits a hundred ahead.
