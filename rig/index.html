⚠️ This is the level's music script, and it is editable here (0163). Change what a section is with the dropdown, where it opens with the number or by dragging the blue line on the strip, and + / × to add or remove one. Every edit reaches the game's own mixer on the next tick, over its own 1.6 second ramp, landing on the bar line (0117). ← → on a focused handle nudges one bar, the smallest move a listener can hear.
⚠️ The order and the count are free (0158). A level may open at surge and drop away, name a section twice, or hold one arrangement for its whole length — nothing asserts what shape a level's music should be (0161). The only bound is that a section outlasts the ramp that opens it, or its arrival never finishes.
⚠️ It belongs to THIS level and no other. Each level keeps its own edits while you switch between them, and copy this moment prints the script as the sections: array it would be pasted back into src/content/levels.ts as, naming the level.
⚠️ The opening entry cannot be moved or removed — a level's music starts where the level starts — but it can be renamed, which is how a level opens at its loudest.
⚠️ The two boundaries after the script have no handle at all. Where the boss arrives is level design rather than a music number, and boss→bossPeak is keyed to the boss's health and not to a clock — so a handle that moved either in time would be a control over something time does not decide.

live is read off the mixer's own GainNode, not modelled — so it cannot disagree with what the speakers are doing. target is the ladder's rung × the theme's multiplier × the aura's ceiling.
⚠️ The faders are ABSOLUTE, not a trim. Touch one and that layer is held there whatever the rung says — so a layer this rung has closed can be dragged up and heard, which is the point. follow hands one layer back; hand it all back returns the lot. A held layer follows a rung change immediately rather than over the mixer's 1.6 second ramp, so judge a transition with nothing held.
⚠️ Stopped means the LEVEL is not playing, and whatever you raise is (0165). Pause, then drag any fader: that layer sounds, on its own, and nothing follows the ladder underneath it. The level clock stays exactly where you stopped it, so the rung you are listening to cannot move. This used to need three gestures — silence everything, find the fader, drag it — and one drag reading 1.55 into total silence is what it did instead.
⚠️ alone is the one-click version, per row: this layer at the loudest the ladder ever takes it here, every other one at zero. Click it again, or everything back, to hand the mixer its mix. It is this place's version of the layer — Ember Nebula's engine is not level one's (0128) — and it is the game's own mixer with faders moved rather than a second player, so the layer is exactly where the bar says it is.
⚠️ pan starts where LAYER_PAN put it. The game's own limit is 0.65 either side (0118) and the fader goes to 1.0 on purpose — past the limit is a question, not a setting.
