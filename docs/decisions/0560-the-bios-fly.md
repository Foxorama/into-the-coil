# 0560 — The bios fly

**Accepted 2026-10-07.** Item 1 of the queue in
[the hangar family, reviewed](../reports/the-hangar-family-reviewed-2026-10-07.md). Amends the `bio`
line [0513](0513-the-pilot-flies.md) put on `GolferRow`.

## The ask

> *"the bio's for the pilots need to get rewritten to be more piloty and less golfy - the same flavour
> can run through them, but the current bios make no sense for a space shooting pilot."*

## The rule

**A pilot's bio says who they are as a pilot.** Each one keeps what its first version was about and
says it in a cockpit:

| pilot | kept | now about |
|---|---|---|
| Feather Fade | the kites over the Ngong Hills, the feather, the fade | reading solar wind, and a slow drift through crossfire |
| Huang-Woo Hook | naming a thing by its sound, the hook, Gwangalli | flying by ear, naming each gun by its report, and a hook under the bridge |
| Longshot Larry | three titles, the dented thing on the mantel, two kids and a kelpie, the road train | long-haul records, and a wagon that towed a road train through a meteor storm |
| Backspin Bo | spin, the coffee, losing to their own spin | spin on every shot, coffee named for barrel rolls, a dogfight lost to their own ricochet |
| The Marmot | the bar on tips, the trade tents, the jar | riding the Thunderbolt out when the jar is full |

## Why

The names stay. *Feather Fade* and *Backspin Bo* carry the fiction across from the predecessor
([0020](0020-the-fiction-transfers-the-code-does-not.md)), and the review asked for the bios, not the
names. The rescue lines already passed this test under
[0426](0426-the-finale-is-the-fight-going-on.md), so a bio is the last place a golf term was the point.

**No guard.** This is copy. It is checked on the hangar's pilot card at every size the layout guard
holds, and `tests/layout.browser.test.ts` fails a card that pushes the screen into a scroll.

## What is owed

A read of them on the hangar.
