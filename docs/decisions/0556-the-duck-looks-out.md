# 0556 — The duck looks out

**Accepted 2026-10-06.** A play of [0555](0555-the-duck-has-a-bill.md) on its branch preview. Amends
0555's MMXXVI. The booth is unchanged.

## The ask

> *"Lower the duck a bit so they're head is visible without being blocked by the stand. also the neck
> angle is really weird, the beak is kind of off a little on alignment and the duck is looking off to the
> side oddly. I'm also not sure about the anime eyes, they might be a bit too much"*

The screenshot that came with it had the neck's two edges traced. 0555's S leaned the head off its own
body.

## The rule

| | |
|---|---|
| **where they stand** | two units lower behind the booth: `at` on their row is three up from the counter's middle, where it was five. At five the beret sat in the awning's scallops. Cosmo's row keeps five, because the stall's awning is not scalloped and Cosmo's place is theirs (0554) |
| **the neck** | straight up out of the helmet's ring under the head, a little narrower at the top, and the far side in shade. The S is gone |
| **facing out** | the duck faces the player, with the head centred over the neck. Two passes at a gentler three-quarter turn kept the eyes looking straight out while the bill went out to the side: *"the bill is sitting too right on the face still, doesn't look correctly proportioned for where the eyes are looking"*. A face turned two ways at once reads wrong however each part is drawn, so the bill turned to match the eyes and the brow that sloped into a side-on bill went |
| **the bill** | centred under the eyes, seen a little from above: a broad scoop wider than it is deep, narrow where the feathers meet it in an arch, flaring to a flat round front edge. A long oval read as a snout, and the side-on paddle before it as *"too pointy"*. It has a lit top and two nostril dots, and the lower half is a thin crescent under the front with its corners turned up into a smile |
| **its outline** | thin, in the bill's own darker ink, never the black line the head wears: *"the black outlining around the bill is weird"* |
| **the black mouth** | *"there's a large black 'mouth' line on the bottom half of the bill"*. It was a defect, not a drawing. The upper's outline was stroked after its highlight and shade, and a canvas stroke outlines the last path traced, so it outlined the shade strip along the bill's foot instead of the bill. The upper is now traced again before it is stroked. It is the same defect as the apron's black blob in 0555, a `stroke()` after a clip, and both were found by photographing the bust at 560 pixels |
| **the eyes and cheeks** | small dark beads, level either side of the bill's root, one glint each. They replace 0555's big two-glint ovals. A pink cheek sits out past each eye, now that both cheeks face the player |

## Why

**On the row, not in the drawing.** The figure's place against the counter is the row's since 0554.
Moving the drawing down inside its box would cut the suit off at the box's foot. Moving the box keeps the
whole drawing, and the counter covers what it always covered.

**No guard.** It is a picture, photographed at both shipped sizes with `scripts/shot-menus.mjs`.
