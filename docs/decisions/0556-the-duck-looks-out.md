# 0556 — The duck looks out

**Accepted 2026-10-06.** A play of [0555](0555-the-duck-has-a-bill.md) on its branch preview. Amends
0555's MMXXVI. The booth is unchanged.

## The ask

> *"Lower the duck a bit so they're head is visible without being blocked by the stand. also the neck
> angle is really weird, the beak is kind of off a little on alignment and the duck is looking off to the
> side oddly. I'm also not sure about the anime eyes, they might be a bit too much"*

The screenshot that came with it had the neck's two edges traced. 0555's S leaned the head off its own
body.

## The rule

| | |
|---|---|
| **where they stand** | two units lower behind the booth: `at` on their row is three up from the counter's middle, where it was five. At five the beret sat in the awning's scallops. Cosmo's row keeps five, because the stall's awning is not scalloped and Cosmo's place is theirs (0554) |
| **the neck** | straight up out of the helmet's ring under the head, a little narrower at the top, and the far side in shade. The S is gone |
| **the turn** | gentler. The head is centred over the neck and the brow is smaller, so the duck looks out with the face towards the pad rather than off to the side |
| **the bill** | comes out of the middle of the brow, level with the line of the eyes, and is shorter for the gentler turn. It is a broad paddle, as deep at its tip as at its root, with a full round tip: *"the duck bill is too pointy still"*. It has a lit top plane, a nostril and the nail. The lower half is laid over the upper's foot and outlined only round its outside, and the two meet in a thin crease in the bill's own ink that turns up at the corner into a smile |
| **the black mouth** | *"there's a large black 'mouth' line on the bottom half of the bill"*. It was a defect, not a drawing. The upper's outline was stroked after its highlight and shade, and a canvas stroke outlines the last path traced, so it outlined the shade strip along the bill's foot instead of the bill. With the two halves' own outlines beside it, that read as a mouth. The upper is now traced again before it is stroked. It is the same defect as the apron's black blob in 0555, a `stroke()` after a clip, and both were found by photographing the bust at 560 pixels |
| **the eyes** | small dark beads either side of the bill's root, one glint each. They replace 0555's big two-glint ovals |

## Why

**On the row, not in the drawing.** The figure's place against the counter is the row's since 0554.
Moving the drawing down inside its box would cut the suit off at the box's foot. Moving the box keeps the
whole drawing, and the counter covers what it always covered.

**No guard.** It is a picture, photographed at both shipped sizes with `scripts/shot-menus.mjs`.
