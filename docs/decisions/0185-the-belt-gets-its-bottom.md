# 0185 — The Belt gets its bottom, and both halves of its brief get heard

**Accepted 2026-08-20.** Four gains, one re-voice and three rungs. Saurian Belt's known-adrift list
goes from **six entries to one**.

> *"Go deep on making saurian a eurobeat style with some deep bassy drums so we can get a mix up of
> modern eurobeat and older style jurassic inspired music."*

## The rules

**Saurian Belt's supersaw pad does not sound below 200 Hz.** Its sub-octave doubling is up an octave,
halved and highpassed; the six saw voices are untouched.

**The place states its own `approach`, `boss` and `bossPeak`.** `toll` and `dread` carry the primeval
half; `drive` carries the floor.

## ⚠️ The answer was a re-voice, and [0181](0181-the-floor-has-a-bottom.md) said so two days ago

0181 was handed *"deeper eurobeat notes"*, added a floor tom, and then measured that it had bought
**+0.004** of the share under 300 Hz. It named the reason in one line — *"there is no room at the
bottom, a chord pad is already standing in it"* — priced the two levers that would work, and handed
them back for an ear.

| the pad, at `push` | |
|---|---|
| over `sub` **in `sub`'s own window** | **+7.6 dB** |
| over `drone` | +12.1 dB |
| loudest layer in the place, by | 7 dB |
| measured centroid | **108 Hz** |
| highpass | **none at all** |

⚠️ **IT WAS A TRIANGLE AT OCTAVE 0 WITH A GAIN OF 0.34, AND ITS OWN COMMENT SAID WHAT IT WAS FOR** —
*what makes the chord felt rather than only heard.* That is a defensible thing for a pad to do and it
is the wrong thing for **this** pad to do: a eurobeat floor is felt through the kick and the bass, and
a chord standing in that register is the one arrangement the genre does not have.

## What moved, and every number is the room the pad gave up

| | from | to |
|---|---|---|
| the pad's bottom voice | octave 0, gain 0.34, no highpass | **octave 1, gain 0.16, highpass 200 Hz** |
| the kick | 0.4, 132 → 32 Hz, 0.42 s | **0.5, 132 → 30 Hz, 0.46 s** |
| the offbeat stab — *the* hi-NRG signature | 0.24 | **0.30** |
| the floor tom 0181 added | 0.32 | **0.44** |

⚠️ **0181's CEILING OF 0.32 ON THE TOM WAS NOT A PROPERTY OF THE TOM.** It measured that past 0.32,
`saurian/approach/drive` went a whole role adrift, and called it *"a bound rather than a taste."* It
was a bound **while the pad was in the way**. With `chords` out of the `low` window the same drum sits
at 0.44 and `drive` is clear.

## ⚠️ Five of the six adrift entries were the two halves of the brief

The file's own header says the place is *a cross between ancient jurassic and eurobeat techno trance*,
and that the two are held apart by register: the primeval material melodic and on top, the hi-NRG
material rhythmic underneath. **The measurement said neither was arriving.**

| | was | now |
|---|---|---|
| `approach/toll` — the bell | −9.9 under its role | cleared |
| `approach/dread` — the thing that noticed you | −9.3 | cleared |
| `boss/dread` | −8.3 | cleared |
| `surge/drive` — the eurobeat lead | −6.0 | cleared |
| `run/groove` | — | **never adrift**, and went with [0184](0184-the-measurement-reads-the-place.md) |

⚠️ **THE LIFTS ARE IN THE LADDER AND NOT IN `mix`, AND THAT IS MEASURED RATHER THAN STYLISTIC.** `mix`
is one number per layer for the whole place, so lifting `drive` for `surge` lifts it in the fight too —
tried at `mix.drive` 1.7, and the boss lost `frenzy` and `wraith` to it. A rung-shaped problem takes
[0162](0162-a-place-has-its-own-ladder.md)'s rung-shaped lever, and
[0176](0176-the-re-based-mix-is-the-mix.md)'s approved balance is left alone.

## ⚠️ And the guard refused the obvious genre move, correctly

The eurobeat gesture before a drop is the **breakdown** — thin the kit, let the pad and the vocal
carry a bar, slam back in. The first version of this dropped `engine` to 0.9 at `approach`.
[0167](0167-a-build-does-not-duck.md) failed it immediately: **a boundary only ever adds**, and a
carried layer getting quieter is a duck whatever it is in aid of.

⚠️ **WHAT REPLACED IT IS THE SAME INTENT IN THE ONLY DIRECTION THE ARRANGEMENT PERMITS** — the bell
and the dread come UP to meet the kit rather than the kit coming down. **The player kept 0167 by name
in the same breath as asking for this**, so it is a floor and not an obstacle. A real breakdown would
need `RUNG_CLOSES`, which is an arrangement change and a different decision.

⚠️ **AND THE CLOSEST PAIR IS THE NUMBER TO WATCH.** `weigh-apart --rung=run` puts Saurian Belt and The
Black Heart at **3.2 dB** against 0147's 3.0 floor. A deeper bottom is a thing both places have, so
this pass moved them together; it passes, and it is the tightest pair in the table. **The Black Heart
is the next place to be worked on and this is the constraint it will hit first.**

## ⚠️ The drums have no probe, and [0019](0019-a-probe-must-be-seen-to-apply.md) asks that be said

The kick, the stab and the tom are gains, and nothing asserts a gain. What they buy is the share under
300 Hz, which is held as a **floor** the place already cleared — so a break that shrank them would go
green, correctly. 0181 recorded the same thing about the tom it added. **The two breaks that exist are
the pad and the three rungs**, and both redden 0164.

## ⚠️ This is a model and the verdict is an ear

[0027](0027-measure-the-picture-not-the-model.md). Every number above is a model quantity. What is
claimed is that the two halves of the brief are no longer under the kit and that the bottom is a drum
rather than a chord — not that the place now sounds the way the ask meant. `npm run dash`.
