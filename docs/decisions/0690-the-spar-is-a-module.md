# 0690 — The spar is a module

**Accepted 2026-10-26.** From the play of 2026-10-26. Amends [0680](0680-depth-and-life.md)'s spar.

## The ask

> *"the new art on the right outside the space station still looks blocky and basic as well."*

## What was there

0680's spar, past the bay: a flat rectangle with three rows of dots for windows, a half-ellipse for a dish and a
girder with a zigzag on it — a box on a stick, read at the stand's camera as exactly that.

## The rule

**The spar is a module on a truss.** In `paintSpar` (`src/render/port-bake.ts`), in the piece's own units:

- a boxed truss — two chords, a web of diagonals, a flange where it meets the box's edge and a cable run along
  the top chord;
- a drum: a cylinder lit from above and dark under, its hull in panels with seams, a band of lit windows with
  the light they throw under them, a docking collar standing proud of the far end with four lights round it, a
  radiator slung under the drum, a dish on top tilted toward the room, a mast with the beacon on it, and running
  lights red to port and green to starboard.

The piece's box, its place past the bay and its plane (0680) are unchanged, so nothing the stand's guards read
moves.

## What guards it

Nothing: it is a picture. **Photographed** at 1280×720 and 1920×1080 on every tab.

## Owed

A look, and whether the shuttle (0680) wants the same pass.

## Rollback

None owed ([0001](0001-revertability-not-risk-rating.md)).
