# 0555 — The duck has a bill

**Accepted 2026-10-06.** A look at Paint & Parts after [0550](0550-every-tab-has-a-keeper.md). Redraws
MMXXVI and their booth. Nothing else moves.

## The ask

> *"let's update the space duck visuals and stand visuals to make them cooler. the space duck needs to be
> cuter and have a proper bill and not a hamburger mouth, also needs a neck and stuff as well. it can look
> much better than it currently does"*

## What was measured first

Off `scripts/shot-menus.mjs` at 1280x720 on `main`, and a scratch rig drawing the bust at 40, 80 and 320
pixels and the booth at twenty pixels a unit. 0550's duck faced straight out. Its bill was two stacked
ellipses with an outline round each, and that reads as a bun, a patty and a bun. The head sat straight on
the collar ring with no neck. The booth was flat rectangles: stripes with a drip each, two plain tins, a
spray can, and a wire circle for a rim.

## Why it is built the way it is

**One drawing, as 0550 had it.** The plate's portrait and the figure behind the counter are still one
painter (`paintMmxxvi`), because the bust's box and place on the row are unchanged. The bottom eleven
hundredths of it sit behind the counter, which is why the brushes stand up out of the pocket rather than
lying in it.

**Short drips over the middle, on purpose.** The booth is drawn over its keeper. 0550's drips ran across
the helmet, and a long run there reads as a crack in the glass.

**Every ink is still a palette role.** The high-contrast palette answers all of it. Its duck is pink, as
0550's was, because the feathers are the hazard ink. That is unchanged here, and it was checked in a
photograph rather than assumed.

**No guard.** Nothing here is an invariant. It is a picture, photographed at both shipped sizes.
